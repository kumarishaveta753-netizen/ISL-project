Beginner-friendly guide to your tasks in ISL Connect
1. The big picture (read this first)
ISL Connect is a website. A person opens it, turns on their camera, and signs in Indian Sign Language (ISL). The computer watches the hands, guesses which sign was made, and shows it as text (and maybe speaks it). The website also has a Learning page (to learn signs with videos), a Practice mode (the camera checks if you copied a sign correctly) and a Progress tracker (shows what you have learned).
It only needs a normal camera � no gloves or special hardware.
Careful: Important scope rule: the first version (called the MVP � Minimum Viable Product, the smallest working version) supports only about 10�20 verified signs. We do NOT claim it translates every ISL sentence. Sign languages have their own grammar and also use the face and body, not just hands.

### 3. Step-by-Step Task Guide



#### Step 1: Write Test Cases

Before testing the application, write a spreadsheet list of test cases detailing what should happen:



| Test ID | Page / Feature | What You Do (Steps) | Expected Result | Pass / Fail |

|---|---|---|---|---|

| TC01 | Camera | Click "Start Camera" button | Webcam preview opens and shows user | PASS |

| TC02 | Camera | Click "Deny" on permission prompt | Show user-friendly error banner | PASS |

| TC03 | Communication | Show 'HELLO' sign to camera | Word 'HELLO' appears in transcript box | PASS |

| TC04 | Confirmation | Low light sign attempt | Modal pop-up appears asking to confirm | PASS |

| TC05 | Speech | Click "Listen" button | Computer speaks transcript aloud | PASS |



#### Step 2: Perform Real-World Testing & Edge Cases

Test the website under tricky real-world situations:

* **Lighting:** Test in bright room light, dim room light, and backlighting.

* **Distance:** Test sitting close to the camera ($0.5\text{m}$) and sitting far away ($2\text{m}$).

* **User Variation:** Have different people perform the signs to see if recognition works for everyone.

* **Browsers:** Test on Chrome, Firefox, and Microsoft Edge.



#### Step 3: Write Clear Bug Reports

When something breaks, write a clear bug report and assign it to the right person:



```text

=====================================================

BUG REPORT ID: BUG_004

Where: Practice Page

Assigned To: Sweety (Frontend)

Severity: High



Steps to Reproduce:

1. Open the Practice Page.

2. Select the sign 'WATER'.

3. Click 'Start Camera' and then immediately click 'Stop Camera'.



Expected Result: Camera feed closes safely.

Actual Result: The page freezes and video screen turns white.

Screenshot: [Attached image bug_004.png]

=====================================================

```



**Bug Owner Reference:**

#### Step 4: Create the Presentation Slides (15 Slides)

Build a 15-slide PowerPoint deck following this outline:

1. **Title Slide:** ISL Connect – Bridging Communication with Indian Sign Language.

2. **Team Slide:** Member names and roles (Pooja, Shaveta, Madhu, Sweety, Meenakshi).

3. **Problem Statement:** Lack of accessible translation and learning tools for ISL users.

4. **Proposed Solution:** Hardware-free web application utilizing computer vision.

5. **Scope & MVP Boundaries:** Word-level translation supporting 10–20 verified signs.

6. **System Architecture:** How Frontend, Flask Backend, MediaPipe AI, and SQLite work together.

7. **Dataset & ML Pipeline:** Data sources, MediaPipe 21 landmark extraction, and model accuracy.

8. **Communication Module:** Real-time sign recognition, speech features, and confirmation workflow.

9. **Learning & Practice Modules:** Lessons, instructions, and camera practice feedback.

10. **Progress Tracker:** SQLite database stats dashboard.

11. **Testing Results:** Test accuracy tables across lighting, distance, and browsers.

12. **Limitations:** Dynamic movement limits, background noise sensitivity, scope boundaries.

13. **Future Roadmap:** Sentence-level translation, full sentence grammar integration, mobile app.

14. **Conclusion & References:** Key takeaways and citation links.

15. **Q&A Slide:** Thank you & Questions.



#### Step 5: Record a Backup Video & Demo Script

2. Your role in simple words
You are the quality checker and the storyteller. First you try to break the app (testing) so problems are found before the judges find them. Then you present the project honestly and confidently (slides, demo, judge questions).
Example: before a restaurant opens, someone tastes every dish and checks every table. That is you. Later you also introduce the restaurant to the guests.
Tip: Good testers are careful and honest. Finding a bug is a win for the team, not a criticism of a person.
