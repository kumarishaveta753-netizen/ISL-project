# ISL Connect – Beginner's Guide for Shaveta

## Role: Data Lead, Research, Content & Team Management



---



### 1. Welcome & Simple Project Overview

Welcome to ISL Connect! Our project helps people communicate using Indian Sign Language (ISL), learn signs through short lessons, and practice them using an ordinary computer camera.



**Important Rule (Scope):**

Our first version (MVP) focuses on **10 to 20 verified ISL signs** only. We are not doing full sentence translation.



**Your Job in 2 Sentences:**

You are the content expert and truth-keeper of the team. You research authentic ISL signs, find and organize high-quality datasets for Madhu's AI model, write easy-to-understand learning guides for Sweety's UI, and help Pooja keep track of daily team tasks.



---



### 2. Tools You Will Use & Why

* **Google Sheets / Microsoft Excel:** To create organized tables listing our 10-20 signs, source links, and lesson text.

* **Kaggle / Roboflow Universe:** Websites where you can search for and download free open-source sign language image/video datasets.

* **Google Drive / Dropbox:** To store raw dataset folders, video clips, and document files securely.

* **Trello / Google Sheets:** To manage daily team checklists alongside Pooja.



---



### 3. Step-by-Step Task Guide



#### Step 1: Select & Verify the 10-20 ISL Signs

1. Pick 10 to 20 signs from simple daily categories:

   * **Greetings:** Hello, Thank You, Welcome

   * **Emergency/Help:** Help, Doctor, Water, Food

   * **Simple Responses:** Yes, No, Please

   * **Numbers:** 1, 2, 3, 4, 5

2. Verify that these signs are **genuine Indian Sign Language (ISL)** and NOT American Sign Language (ASL).

3. Create a master Google Sheet named `ISL_Master_Signs`:

   * Column A: Sign Name (e.g., `HELLO`)

   * Column B: Hands Used (1 Hand or 2 Hands)

   * Column C: Is there movement? (Yes/No)

   * Column D: Source Link (Where you verified it)

   * Column E: Status (Verified ISL)



#### Step 2: Find & Download Datasets for Madhu

1. Go to Kaggle.com or Universe.roboflow.com and search for *"Indian Sign Language Dataset"*.

2. Check the license: Make sure it is free to use for academic/project purposes.

3. Check the images: Ensure the pictures are clear, well-lit, and correctly labeled.

4. Download the folder and organize it on Google Drive like this:

   ```text

   ISL_Dataset/

   ├── HELLO/

   │   ├── img1.jpg

   │   └── img2.jpg

   ├── THANK_YOU/

   │   ├── img1.jpg

   │   └── img2.jpg

   └── WATER/

   ```

5. Share the folder link with **Madhu**.



#### Step 3: Write Learning Page Content for Sweety

For every sign in our project, write a simple card in a document or spreadsheet for Sweety to put on the Learning page:



* **Sign Label:** HELLO

* **English Meaning:** A friendly greeting when meeting someone.

* **Hindi Meaning:** नमस्ते / अभिवादन

* **Step-by-Step Instructions:** Raise your dominant hand to temple level with palm facing outward, and wave gently side-to-side.

* **Difficulty:** Easy

* **Video Reference Link:** (Add link to a short clip showing how to perform it)



#### Step 4: Write the Project Report

You will draft the main text for the final project report:

1. **Problem Statement:** Why sign language translation and learning tools are needed.

2. **Existing vs Proposed System:** How ISL Connect works using a web camera without expensive hardware gloves.

3. **Dataset Details:** Where we got our dataset, how many samples we used, and known scope limits (e.g., word-level recognition only).
